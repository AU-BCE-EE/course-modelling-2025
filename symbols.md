# Symbols
Below is a list of symbols we will commonly use in course materials.
It is difficult to devise a unique set of symbols across multiple fields, and despite our efforts, some are probably not unique and others are probably not clear.
Feel free to send other suggestions and to let us know of cases where we do not follow this list.

1. $Q$ Thermal energy, typically in J.
2. $\dot Q$ Rate of change in thermal energy, typically $\text{J} \text{s}^{-1}$.
