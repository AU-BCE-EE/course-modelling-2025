
A 3D printer prints its material on a heat bed. This heat bed is made of aluminium and should be kept at a constant temperature that matches the melting temperature of the printing material. 
In this exercises lets say we have polylactic acid and the heat bed temperture needs to be a 60 degrees in the middle of the heat bed. 
The dimensions of the heat bed is 20 x 20 cm and with a thickness of 0.3 cm. 
It is heated at all four edges such that the temperature is 100 degrees at the edges. 
Heat is lost from the heat bed surface on only one side. 
Make a 2D model in python and predict how long time it takes to reach 60 degrees in the middle of the heat bed assuming that the heat bed to start with is the same temperature as the room air (20 deg C). 
A plot of the heat bed center temperature over time should be good enough for solving this exercise.
