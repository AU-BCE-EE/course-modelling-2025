# Aeration

Water can be aerated (or more accurately, oxygenated) by introducing a continuous flow of small air bubbles at the bottom of a tank or other vessel.
Mass transfer rate depends on the mass transfer coefficient $k_L$ ($\text{m} \text{s}^{-1}$) and the bubble area ($\text{m}^2$) which is typically expressed on a volumetric basis (so $\text{m}^2 \text{m}^{-3}$).
