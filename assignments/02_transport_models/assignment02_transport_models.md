# Assignment 2. Transport models

1. Some medications can be administered through transdermal delivery systems.
Patients wear a patch on their skin, and the medication diffuses through a polymer layer to the skin surface, and then from there through the skin and into the bloodstream. 
Assume a polymer patch is round with a 5 cm diameter.
It contains a source of 50 mg of medication in a 0.1 mm thick layer.
A 0.2 mm polymer layer between the source and skin controls the rate of drug delivery.
Assuming diffusivity is 3 x 10⁻¹² (3E-12) m² s⁻¹, can you develop a model for drug delivery rate (mg s⁻¹) over time?

2. How long will it take for an ice cube to melt once it is exposed to room temperature?
