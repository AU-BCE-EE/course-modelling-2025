# Exercise 1. Simple transport and microbial models

1. A denitrifying wood chip bioreactor is just a trench in the ground filled with wood chips.
Nitrate (NO₃⁻) in water draining from agricultural fields is removed by bacterial denitrification, with wood organic matter as the electron donor and carbon source.
The nitrate is converted to relatively inert dinitrogen gas (N₂) (or at least that is the intent).
Can you write mass balance equations for wood and the nitrogen compounds?
If you assume a first-order reaction for the rate of wood degradation, can you write an expression for the denitrification rate?
What simplifications have you made?

2. Fish transport dissolved oxygen from water into their bloodstream through gills.
Early work on the topic suggests that most of the resistance for this transport comes from a water film outside the gills. 
An increase in water flow over gills might reduce effective film thickness from 50 down to 20 μm.
How fast can oxygen diffuse in for these two scenarios?
Bulk oxygen concentration depends on temperature and consumption rate, but you may assume it is 10 mg L⁻¹, which might be the case for cool, clean water.
In the blood, free oxygen rapidly reacts with hemoglobin.
You can assume a diffusivity of 2 x 10⁻⁹ m⁻² s⁻¹.
